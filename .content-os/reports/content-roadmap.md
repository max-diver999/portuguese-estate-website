## The order of operations matters more than the list

Three of the site's top five GSC pages already rank on **page one** and convert at **under 0.5% CTR**:

|---|---|---|

`portugal-property-under-300000-euros` gets **6× the CTR** of `property-management-portugal-cost` from a *worse* position. The difference is packaging, not content.

**Publishing 50 new articles onto a site where 134 of 146 titles are truncated in SERPs, every page has two H1s, and 126 pages share 10 hotlinked images would multiply the defect, not the traffic.** Fix the site first; then this plan compounds instead of diluting.

## Strategy: own transaction mechanics, not residency

The residency-led competitors — `globalcitizensolutions.com`, `immigrantinvest.com`, `getgoldenvisa.com` — have far more authority on visa head terms and will keep it. `portugalpropertyinvest.com` runs near-identical positioning ("independent buyer intelligence for foreigners") and is the closest direct rival.

Portuguese Estate's defensible territory is **tax, compliance and transaction mechanics** — where it already ranks (RNAL 6.2, under-300k 8.1, property-management 9.8) and where the competition is thin, because that content is expensive to produce and dates fast.

**All 50 articles below sit inside that territory.** None chase Golden Visa or residency head terms.

## Notes on cluster design

**Cluster G extends the winner.** `areas` scores **75** — the best collection, zero files below 60. The six new micro-areas fill the obvious holes: Porto has only 2 area pages (`matosinhos`, `vila-nova-de-gaia`) despite being the #2 market and the subject of `porto-property-investment-guide`. Ribeira, Foz and Boavista are the three addresses a Porto buyer actually searches.

**Cluster H fixes `compare`, it does not extend it.** `compare` is the weakest collection (65, 14/14 below 75) and already covers 9 countries. Adding a 10th country would deepen the problem. Three of the four new comparisons are **intra-Portugal** — the decision a buyer who has already chosen Portugal actually faces, and the one with genuine lead intent. Only #50 adds a country (#40 sits in cluster C but publishes to `compare`), and it targets the underserved Brazilian segment the site already has a page for.

**Cluster A opens a new audience.** Every existing page addresses a buyer. Sellers are a distinct, high-intent audience the site has zero coverage of — and they convert twice, because a seller with Portuguese proceeds is a strong re-buyer.

**5–10 articles per batch, never more.** Each must clear `qa:full` at exit 0 before the next starts.

### Quality bar for every new article

Written to the standard the corpus is being *raised* to, not the one it is at:

- **Answer-first opener** — 40–60 words, noun-phrase start, one hard statistic, a definition verb
- **≥ 2 citability blocks** — 130–170 words, self-contained, stat-bearing (corpus average today is **0.36 per page**)
- ≥ 4 H2 sections, ≥ 3 tables, ≥ 8 numeric facts, FAQ block, pros/cons, risks/red-flags, buyer scenario
- **Rendered** `<title>` ≤ 60 chars — not just frontmatter
- **Exactly one `<h1>`**
- **A unique, region-correct hero image** — not one of the 10 currently shared across 126 pages
- ≥ 5 internal links, **every one verified to resolve**
- Primary sources cited: AT, INE, AICCOPN, Diário da República

**New articles must not inherit the defects the cleanup is removing.**
