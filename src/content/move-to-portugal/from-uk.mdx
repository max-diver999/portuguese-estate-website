None of them covers buying a house. They will move your furniture into a property nobody told you how to purchase, and the tax on that purchase is the largest single number in a British relocation after the property itself. That is the gap this page fills.

## What Brexit Changed, and What It Did Not

It did not change ownership. Portugal has never applied a nationality condition to residential property, and it did not introduce one. A British buyer registers the same freehold title at the same Conservatória as a German or Portuguese buyer, with the same protections.

It changed the right to stay. Since 2021, UK passport holders are third-country nationals in Schengen terms, which means 90 days in any rolling 180-day period without a visa or residence permit.

| Position | Before 2021 | From 2021 |
|---|---|---|
| Buying property | Unrestricted | Unrestricted, unchanged |
| Time in Portugal | Unlimited, freedom of movement | 90 days in any rolling 180 |
| Working locally | By right | Requires the appropriate permit |
| Healthcare access | EU coordination | Via residence, or private cover as a visitor |
| Purchase tax treatment | Same as any non-resident | Same as any non-resident |

The 90/180 count is checked electronically at the border. The older assumption that it is loosely policed described the era of manually stamped passports and does not describe the current system. If an agent tells you otherwise, treat everything else they say with the same suspicion.

For a second-home owner who wants three months in spring and three in autumn, the arithmetic does not work without a residence permit. That, more than tax, is what pushes British owners toward the D7.

## Which Route Fits

| Route | 2026 test | Presence expected | Capital locked |
|---|---|---|---|
| D7 passive income | About EUR 920 a month for a single applicant | High, genuine residence | None |
| D8 remote work | About EUR 3,680 a month from outside Portugal | Medium to high | None |
| Golden Visa fund | EUR 500,000 for five years | About 7 days a year | Yes |

Both income tests track the Portuguese minimum wage, EUR 920 a month in 2026, with the D8 at four times that figure. The detail is in [the D7 guide](/move-to-portugal/d7-visa/) and [the D8 guide](/move-to-portugal/d8-digital-nomad-visa/).

The Golden Visa is the route British buyers ask about and rarely need. It stopped accepting property in October 2023, and for anyone actually moving, the D7 delivers the same residence rights without EUR 500,000 tied up for five years. See [the Golden Visa guide](/move-to-portugal/golden-visa/) if capital rather than relocation is the point.

## What the House Costs to Buy

| Cost | Rate or basis | On a EUR 400,000 Algarve house |
|---|---|---|
| IMT, non-resident | Flat 7.5% on residential property from 1 September 2026 | EUR 30,000 |
| Stamp duty | 0.8% of price | EUR 3,200 |
| Notary and land registry | Fixed and variable elements | Low four figures |
| Legal fees | Commonly 1% to 1.5% | EUR 4,000 to EUR 6,000 |
| Total acquisition cost | Roughly 9% to 11% of price | About EUR 39,000 to EUR 45,000 |

The flat 7.5% non-resident rate under DL 97/2026 is what a British buyer pays before establishing Portuguese tax residency. A resident buying the same house moves along a progressive scale that starts far lower. The full mechanics are in [IMT for non-residents](/guides/imt-tax-non-resident-portugal-2026/), and the refund route for those who go on to become resident within 24 months is in [the IMT refund guide](/guides/imt-refund-tax-resident-portugal/).

Sequencing matters more than negotiating. On this example, buying after establishing tax residency rather than before is worth more than most buyers achieve by haggling on price.

The transaction itself follows the standard foreign-buyer path: NIF, bank account, independent lawyer, promissory contract, deed. See [buying property in Portugal as a foreigner](/guides/how-to-buy-property-portugal-step-by-step/) and [the step-by-step process](/guides/how-to-buy-property-portugal-step-by-step/). Non-resident lending is covered in [Portugal mortgage rates for foreigners](/guides/portugal-mortgage-rates-foreigners-2026/).

## Tax: the Treaty Does the Work

Once Portuguese tax resident, you are taxed on worldwide income, and the UK Portugal double taxation treaty allocates taxing rights and provides relief. Unlike the American case, there is no saving clause pulling the UK back in on citizenship grounds: British tax follows residence.

The pressure point is pensions. Government service pensions are treated differently from private and workplace pensions under the treaty, and the outcome depends on which you hold. The regime that used to make this simple, NHR, closed to new applicants at the end of 2024. Its replacement, IFICI, applies a flat rate to qualifying Portuguese employment and self-employment income and excludes pensions entirely.

Anyone whose move was modelled on NHR pension treatment is modelling a regime they cannot join. The treaty detail is in [the UK Portugal double taxation treaty guide](/guides/uk-portugal-double-taxation-treaty/), the residency test in [the 183-day guide](/guides/portugal-tax-residency-183-days/), and IFICI in [the NHR replacement guide](/guides/ifici-portugal-nhr-2-tax-regime/).

## The Practical Move, in the Order It Bites

The removal firms cover this ground well, so this is the short version with the two items they tend to underplay.

**Currency comes first and lasts longest.** A British buyer converts sterling to euros at the deposit, again at completion, and then for every subsequent transfer for the rest of the ownership. A 5% move on a EUR 400,000 purchase is EUR 20,000, and Portuguese completion dates slip routinely. Decide whether you are fixing the rate or accepting the exposure, and decide it before the promissory contract rather than in the week of the deed.

**Healthcare has two layers.** Private cover is required for the visa application and buys speed and English-speaking consultations. Once you hold a residence permit and a social security number you can also register with the public system. British state pensioners should check their S1 entitlement position before assuming either route, because it changes what the public system costs them.

**The driving licence has a deadline.** The exchange window runs from your residence date, and it is short. Miss it and you sit a Portuguese test rather than exchanging a valid licence.

**Pets travel on an animal health certificate**, not the old EU pet passport, since 2021. It is issued close to travel and has a validity window, so it is a scheduling problem rather than a paperwork one.

| Item | When to start | Consequence of leaving it |
|---|---|---|
| NIF and Portuguese bank account | Before anything else | Nothing else can proceed |
| Currency plan | Before the promissory contract | Uncontrolled exposure on the largest transfer of your life |
| Health cover | Before the visa application | The application is incomplete |
| Driving licence exchange | Within months of residence | A Portuguese driving test |
| Animal health certificate | Close to travel, to its validity window | The pet does not fly |

| Area | Why | The trade |

| Central and eastern Algarve | The established British market, direct Faro flights, English widely spoken | Faro's winter flight schedule thins sharply |

| Porto and the north | City quality at a discount | Colder, smaller British community |

One correction to the received wisdom. The Algarve is not one market. The central resort strip from Albufeira to Vilamoura keeps a large seasonal population and thins in winter; the eastern towns around Tavira and Olhao hold a resident population all year; and the western coast beyond Lagos is quieter in both directions. British buyers who visited in July and bought in the central strip are the ones most likely to be selling within three years.

The Algarve holds the largest British community in Portugal, and the demand data agrees: [Albufeira](/property-for-sale/albufeira/), [Tavira](/property-for-sale/tavira/), [Vilamoura](/property-for-sale/vilamoura/) and [Lagos](/property-for-sale/lagos/) are the four towns British searches concentrate on. The regional picture is in [the Algarve guide](/guides/algarve-property-investment-guide/).

## Driving Licences, Cars and the NHS Question

Three practical items catch British movers in the first year, and none of them appears in the visa paperwork.

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| UK driving licence | Must be exchanged for a Portuguese one once resident |
| Bringing a car | Possible, with an import process and a tax exemption route |
| NHS access | Ends with UK residence; the S1 covers state pensioners |
| Healthcare in Portugal | SNS access once registered, plus private insurance is common |
| EHIC and GHIC | Travel cover only, not a substitute for residency healthcare |

The licence exchange is administrative but time-bound, and leaving it until the licence expires creates a problem that is far harder to solve than the exchange would have been.

The car is the one worth deciding early. Importing a vehicle as part of a change of residence can qualify for relief from the vehicle tax that otherwise makes imports expensive, but the relief has conditions on prior ownership and on timing relative to the move. Getting the sequence wrong turns a saving into a substantial bill, and the sequence is decided before you ship, not after.

On healthcare, the position for a state pensioner differs from that for a working-age mover, and the S1 route is worth confirming with the UK authority before departure. See [the UK Portugal double taxation treaty guide](/guides/uk-portugal-double-taxation-treaty/) for the tax side of the same transition.

## Bringing Money Across, and the Currency Question

A British household buying in euros with sterling income is running a currency position whether or not they think of it that way, and on a property purchase the sums involved make it material.

| Exposure | Effect |
|---|---|
| Purchase price in euros | The whole sum moves at one rate |
| Deposit at CPCV, balance at escritura | Two conversions, months apart |
| Ongoing costs in euros | Recurring exposure, indefinitely |
| Pension or salary in sterling | Income exposed every month |
| Rental income in euros | A natural hedge, if you let |

On a purchase of a few hundred thousand euros, a few percentage points of movement between the CPCV and the escritura is a five-figure sum, and the gap between the two is typically weeks or months. That is the exposure people notice.

The one they do not notice is the ongoing one. A retired couple spending in euros on a sterling pension carries that position for as long as they live in Portugal, and it does not net out. Whether to hedge any of it is a decision for a regulated adviser rather than for us, but it belongs on the planning list well before the offer.

## Pets, and the Order the Paperwork Has to Happen In

Moving a dog or cat from the UK to Portugal is straightforward and unforgiving of sequence errors. The animal needs a microchip first, then a rabies vaccination after the chip, then a waiting period, then an animal health certificate issued within a defined window before travel. Doing the vaccination before the chip invalidates it and restarts the clock, which is the single most common mistake and costs weeks.

Start the process at least three months before the move, confirm the current requirements with a vet who does export paperwork regularly, and check the carrier's own rules separately, because airline and ferry conditions are stricter than the legal minimum and vary by route and by season.

## Pros and Cons

Advantages:

- Ownership completely unrestricted, with no post-Brexit condition on title
- An established British community with English-speaking services on the Algarve
- Housing costs well below southern England for comparable coastal property
- Direct flights from most UK regional airports, at least in season
- A double taxation treaty that follows residence rather than citizenship

Disadvantages:

- 90 days in any 180 without a residence permit, electronically enforced
- Flat 7.5% non-resident IMT until you establish tax residency
- NHR no longer available, and IFICI does not cover pensions
- Sterling to euro exposure between offer and completion, and on every future transfer
- Winter flight schedules to Faro thin considerably

## Risks and Red Flags

- **"The 90 days is not really enforced."** It described the stamped-passport era. An agent saying it now is either out of date or selling.
- **Any adviser offering NHR.** Closed to new applicants since the end of 2024.
- **Buying before the residence decision.** It fixes you on the flat 7.5% rate for a purchase you could have timed.
- **Sterling exposure left unhedged.** A 5% move between promissory contract and deed on EUR 400,000 is EUR 20,000, and completion dates slip.
- **Agents introducing "their" lawyer.** In Portugal the agent is paid by the seller. Instruct your own.

Checklist before you commit:

- Count your Schengen days accurately for the last 180, before assuming the visa question is theoretical
- Decide the residence route before the purchase, not after
- Get a treaty opinion on your specific pension mix
- Fix the currency, or accept the exposure knowingly
- Diarise the driving licence exchange window from your residence date

## Who This Works For

**The retiring couple on private pensions.** D7 fits comfortably. The work is the pension tax position under the treaty now that NHR has closed, and the sequencing of any purchase against tax residency. The fuller picture is in [retiring in Portugal](/move-to-portugal/retire-in-portugal/).

**The second-home owner who wants more than 90 days.** The honest answer is that there is no halfway option. Either you accept the limit or you take a residence route, with the tax consequences that follow.

**The remote employee.** D8 fits, and Lisbon or Porto rather than the Algarve, on connectivity and community. Rent through the first year.

**The family relocating with children.** School places decide the area before houses do, and that concentrates you on the Cascais line: see [international schools in Portugal](/move-to-portugal/international-schools/).
