Quick Answer: The Algarve is not one market. The golden triangle, the central resort strip, the west and the east price differently, empty differently in winter and suit different buyers. Choosing the stretch matters more than choosing the property, and the flat 7.5% non-resident IMT applies across all of them.

One number frames the whole region. The Algarve is roughly 150 kilometres end to end, and a buyer who says "the Algarve" is describing a coastline longer than the distance from London to Birmingham. Nobody would shop for a house across that span without narrowing it first, and yet most Algarve searches begin exactly there.

Search demand agrees that the Algarve is the centre of the British market in Portugal: `algarve properties buy` runs at 2,400 a month in the UK, `property for sale algarve` at 1,900, and individual towns like Albufeira at 590 and Vilamoura and Tavira at 480 each.

What the portals ranking above this page cannot tell you is which of the four Algarves you are actually looking at. That is what decides whether you have bought a home or a summer.

## The Four Algarves

| Stretch | Towns | Character | Winter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Golden triangle | Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Almancil | Golf resorts, large plots, gated developments | Quiet but staffed and open |
| Central strip | Albufeira, Vilamoura, Quarteira, Carvoeiro | Built around the season, apartments and resort villas | Thins sharply from November |
| West | Lagos, Praia da Luz, Burgau, Sagres | Dramatic coast, smaller towns, surf | Quiet, fewer services |
| East | Faro, Olhao, Tavira, Cabanas | Working towns, Ria Formosa, resident population | The mildest, and the most open |
| Inland | Silves, Monchique, Loule | Countryside, quintas, lowest prices | Cold nights, long drives |

The single most useful question a buyer can answer before viewing anything is which row they are in. It determines the price, the winter, the rental pattern and the resale market, and it is not a question the listing tells you.

## What Each Stretch Costs

| Stretch | Price level, region average = 100 | What drives it |
|---|---|---|
| Golden triangle | Well above 100 | Plot size, golf frontage, gated security, international schools nearby |
| Central strip, front line | Above 100 | Sea view and walkable beach |
| Central strip, set back | Around 100 | The bulk of the apartment stock |
| West | Around 100, with sharp premiums on the coast | Scarcity rather than volume |
| East | Below 100 | Working towns, less foreign demand per square metre |
| Inland | Well below 100 | Land is not the constraint here, condition is |

Read those as relative. Take absolute figures per square metre from the INE series with a date, as set out in [Portugal house prices](/guides/portugal-house-prices/), because published Algarve numbers age fast and portal averages mix asking prices with achieved ones.

Two Algarve-specific cautions on price. Sea view is priced steeply and defined loosely, so establish what is visible from which room in which season before paying for it. And plot size in the golden triangle is a large part of the number, which means the cost per square metre of building is a poor comparison tool there.

## What You Pay on Top

| Cost | Basis | On a EUR 450,000 villa |
|---|---|---|
| IMT, non-resident | Flat 7.5% on residential property from 1 September 2026 | EUR 33,750 |
| Stamp duty | 0.8% of price | EUR 3,600 |
| Notary and registry | Fixed and variable | Low four figures |
| Legal fees | Commonly 1% to 1.5% | EUR 4,500 to EUR 6,750 |
| Total | Roughly 9% to 11% | About EUR 43,000 to EUR 50,000 |

The flat 7.5% rate applies while you are non-resident. A Portuguese tax resident buying the same villa moves onto a progressive scale that starts far lower, and a buyer who becomes resident within 24 months can reclaim the excess. The mechanics are in [IMT for non-residents](/guides/imt-tax-non-resident-portugal-2026/) and [the full cost of buying](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-portugal/).

## What It Costs to Hold

The Algarve carries higher running costs than inland Portugal, for reasons that are physical rather than fiscal.

| Charge | Algarve character |
|---|---|
| IMI | 0.3% to 0.45% of rateable value, set by each municipality |
| AIMI | 0.7% above EUR 600,000 of taxable value per owner, which the golden triangle reaches routinely |
| Condominium | Resort developments with pools, gardens and security carry the highest budgets in Portugal |
| Pool and garden | A private pool is a recurring service contract, not a one-off |
| Salt-air maintenance | Front-line property needs more frequent external work than inland stock |

The condominium budget is the line to interrogate before buying into any resort development. Ask for the last two annual accounts and any approved works not yet billed. A pool refurbishment or a security contract renegotiation lands as a special assessment with a few months of notice.

The running-cost detail is in [Portugal property running costs](/guides/property-management-portugal-cost/).

## Letting It Out

Holiday letting requires an Alojamento Local registration, and the position varies by municipality. Several operate containment zones that restrict new registrations, so the answer for one street is not the answer for the next town.

Two rules follow. Verify the AL position for the specific address before the promissory contract, not after. And if a seller is marketing an existing registration as part of the value, confirm in writing whether it transfers on sale in that municipality, and what happens to the price if it does not.

Seasonality is the other half. Peak-week rates annualise into yields that do not survive contact with the void months, the management fee, IMI, the condominium charge and tax. The licensing framework is in [the Alojamento Local guide](/guides/alojamento-local-license-portugal/).

## New Build or Resale

The Algarve has more new construction than anywhere else in Portugal outside the Lisbon metro, and the choice between new and resale is sharper here than the national picture suggests.

| | New build and off plan | Resale |
|---|---|---|
| Condition risk | Low on delivery, high on completion timing | Known, and surveyable |
| Payment | Staged against construction milestones | One deposit, one completion |
| Energy performance | Materially better, which shows in winter running costs | Older stock is usually poorly insulated |
| Location | Whatever land was available, often set back from the coast | The established front lines were built first |
| Price | A premium over comparable resale | Cheaper per square metre, more work |
| Resale market | Competing against the next phase of the same development | Competing against everything |

The completion risk is the one to price. An off-plan purchase in the Algarve is a commitment against a schedule, and schedules slip. Ask what happens to your staged payments if it does, and check that the answer is in the contract rather than in a conversation.

The energy point is underrated. A poorly insulated Algarve villa is cold and damp from December to February, and the heating cost is a real annual number rather than a footnote. New construction to current standards changes that materially, which is part of what the premium buys.

Off-plan mechanics are covered in [the off-plan property guide](/guides/off-plan-property-portugal-guide/).

## Pros and Cons

Advantages:

- The largest English-speaking community in Portugal, with services to match
- Direct flights from most UK and northern European airports, at least in season
- The mildest winters in mainland Portugal, particularly in the east
- A deep resale market in the central strip, which matters on exit
- Prices below comparable Mediterranean resort coasts

Disadvantages:

- Severe seasonality outside the eastern towns
- Faro's winter flight schedule thins considerably
- Resort condominium budgets are the highest in the country
- AL containment restricts letting in several municipalities
- Prices in the central strip have risen faster than rents since 2020

## Risks and Red Flags

- **Buying on a summer visit.** The most common regret in the region, and a February trip settles it.
- **Sea view sold loosely.** Establish what is visible, from where, and whether a plot in front is developable.
- **AL income assumed, not verified.** Check the municipal position on the address before you commit.
- **Condominium accounts not requested.** Special assessments arrive with little notice and no ceiling.
- **Treating the Algarve as one market.** The east and the central strip are different products at similar prices.

Checklist before you offer:

- Confirm which of the four stretches the property is in, and visit in winter
- Get the AL position for that address in writing from the municipality
- Ask for the last two condominium accounts and any approved works
- Get the current VPT and last year's IMI on the specific property
- Model the purchase at the non-resident rate, then again as a resident

## Who Each Stretch Suits

**Year-round residents and retirees.** The east. Tavira and Olhao keep their populations, services and schools through winter, and the climate is the mildest in mainland Portugal. Our [Tavira](/property-for-sale/tavira/) guide covers the town.

**Holiday-home buyers who will let.** The central strip, subject to the AL position. The season is real, the management infrastructure exists, and the resale market is the deepest in the region. See [Albufeira](/areas/albufeira-property-investment/) and [Vilamoura](/areas/vilamoura-property-investment/).

**Buyers at the top of the market.** The golden triangle, and the comparison set is other European resort markets rather than the rest of Portugal.

**Buyers who want quiet and coast.** The west around [Lagos](/property-for-sale/lagos/), accepting fewer services out of season.

**Buyers optimising for price.** Inland, where condition rather than land is the constraint, and where a renovation budget and a licence queue both belong in the model.

The wider regional analysis, including yields and market history, is in [the Algarve property guide](/guides/algarve-property-investment-guide/).
