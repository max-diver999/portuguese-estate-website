Quick Answer: Mar Adentro is an off-plan waterfront regeneration scheme by Lantia in Faro, of roughly 200 units from about €320,000, with sales from 2026. Faro is the Algarve's administrative capital and airport city rather than a resort, which gives the scheme a year-round local demand base that resort-strip stock does not have.

Faro is the Algarve city that people fly through rather than to, and that is the basis of its investment case. The administrative capital carries a hospital, a university, the regional government and the airport, which produces demand across twelve months rather than three. A waterfront regeneration scheme here is exposed to that base rather than to holiday occupancy alone.

## Why does Faro behave differently from the resort Algarve?

The resort strip from Albufeira to Vilamoura is priced on summer weeks. Faro is priced on employment. The distinction matters most in the shoulder months, when resort occupancy collapses and Faro's does not, because its tenants are working rather than visiting.

| Demand driver | Faro | Resort strip |
|---|---|---|
| Employment base | Hospital, university, airport, government | Hospitality, seasonal |
| Letting pattern | Twelve-month contracts common | Peak-season short lets |
| Winter occupancy | Steady | Sharply lower |
| Buyer pool at resale | Local plus international | Predominantly international |
| Price per square metre | Below resort equivalents | Premium for beachfront |

The Algarve took 42.4% of non-resident deal value in 2025, but that money concentrates in the resort corridor. Faro's pricing reflects local wages more than foreign budgets, which is why entry at around €320,000 buys more here than the same money buys twenty minutes west.

## What does the Ria Formosa setting add to due diligence?

Waterfront in Faro means the Ria Formosa, a protected lagoon system with its own regulatory regime. That protection is an amenity and a constraint at once, and both belong in the diligence.

- **Confirm the environmental licensing** for the scheme, not just the construction licence. Protected-area works carry additional approvals that can move timelines.
- **Check flood and coastal-erosion mapping** for the parcel. Ground-floor units and parking levels are the exposure.
- **Establish what is permitted between the building and the water.** Protection often prevents future construction, which supports the view, but confirm it rather than assume it.
- **Ask about insurance.** Flood cover in coastal-lagoon locations is priced individually and is worth quoting before, not after, the deposit.
- **Check the PDM and any coastal management plan** for the parish, and search câmara bulletins for the last 24 months.

Read [Faro property investment](/areas/faro-property-investment/) for the city's wider market and [due diligence for Portuguese property](/guides/due-diligence-portugal-property/) for the registry sequence.

## How do the off-plan protections work here?

Every payment made before the licença de utilização exists must be covered by a bank guarantee or insurance under Decreto-Lei 67/2003, in the amount paid. On a scheme of roughly 200 units delivered in phases, the guarantee must track your cumulative payments rather than the phase as a whole.

The points that fail in practice are the same everywhere: a guarantee naming the marketing brand rather than the buyer, an amount that was never reissued after the second tranche, a longstop expressed as a construction phase rather than a calendar date, and a seller entity whose NIPC does not match the land registry. Check all four before the first transfer, using the [CPCV guide](/guides/cpcv-promissory-contract-portugal/) and the [deposit guide](/guides/portugal-property-deposit-guide-cpcv/).

## What does a non-resident pay at this entry level?

At €320,000 a non-resident completing after 1 September 2026 pays flat 7.5% IMT under DL 97/2026, which is €24,000, plus €2,560 stamp duty and roughly €6,400 to €9,600 in legal and registry costs, so cash to close sits near €353,000 to €356,000.

AIMI does not arise at this value for a single owner, since the €600,000 allowance applies to taxable value and the VPT will sit below the price. Annual IMI runs 0.3% to 0.45% of VPT depending on the municipal rate. The full cost sequence is in [cost of buying property](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-portugal/).

## How should a phased scheme of this size be timed?

Roughly 200 units are not delivered at once, and the phase you buy into determines what you live beside for the first years of ownership. Early phases complete into an active construction site; later phases complete into a finished neighbourhood but commit capital for longer and carry more exposure to a promoter running out of momentum.

| Phase position | Advantage | Exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Best price, first choice of orientation | Years beside active works |
| Middle | Some site completed, price still below final | Longstop risk on remaining phases |
| Late | Finished surroundings, visible product | Highest price, longest wait |

Ask for the phasing plan in writing and match it against the construction licence, because a promoter's marketing sequence and the licensed sequence are not always the same document. Where a later phase sits between your unit and the water, that is the single most important line on the plan.

The capital gains position is ordinary: 50% of the net gain at 14.5% to 48%, with the €24,000 IMT, the stamp duty and any documented works deductible when you eventually sell, as set out in the [mais-valias guide](/guides/mais-valias-portugal-capital-gains/).

## Who should consider Mar Adentro?

The scheme suits a buyer who wants Algarve exposure with tenant demand that survives the winter, at an entry price the resort corridor no longer offers. It suits poorly a buyer whose priority is a beachfront resort address, and poorly anyone unwilling to work through protected-area licensing before committing.

For the regional comparison, read [the Algarve property investment guide](/guides/algarve-property-investment-guide/); for the alternative of buying finished stock instead, [new-build versus resale](/compare/new-build-vs-resale-property-portugal/).

## What to verify before paying a deposit

1. DL 67/2003 guarantee naming you, covering cumulative payments to date
2. Environmental licensing for works in the Ria Formosa protected area
3. Flood and coastal-erosion mapping for the specific parcel
4. Insurance quotation for flood cover, obtained before the deposit
5. Calendar longstop date with refund mechanics in the CPCV
6. Seller NIPC matching land registry and construction licence
7. Phasing plan, so you know what is built beside you and when
8. Draft condominium budget for a scheme of this size
