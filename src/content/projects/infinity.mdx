Quick Answer: Infinity is a completed Vanguard Properties high-rise at Sete Rios in Lisbon, with apartments from about €650,000. As a finished tower it carries no off-plan risk, but it does carry the running-cost profile of a high-rise: lifts, plant, common climate control and a reserve fund shared across many floors rather than a handful of units.

Infinity is the counterexample to the assumption that a tower is just a taller apartment building. Vertical schemes concentrate mechanical plant, and the cost of running lifts, pumps, pressurisation and common ventilation falls on the condominium every month whether the market is rising or not. That is the number an investor should chase before the view.

## What does a high-rise condominium actually cost to run?

High-rise service charges in Lisbon typically run above those of a low-rise building of equivalent quality, because the plant list is longer and the maintenance contracts are not optional. Lifts require certified inspection, pressurisation systems serve fire compliance, and a tower cannot defer either without losing its occupancy compliance.

| Cost driver | Why a tower differs | What to request |
|---|---|---|
| Lift maintenance and inspection | Multiple cars, mandatory certification | Contract and last inspection report |
| Pressurisation and fire systems | Statutory in tall buildings | Compliance certificate |
| Common climate control | Larger common areas, lobbies | Energy line in the accounts |
| Facade access for cleaning and repair | Cradle or rope access | Reserve fund balance |
| Reserve fund adequacy | One facade job is very large | Two years of accounts plus AGM minutes |

The reserve fund is the single most informative figure. A tower with a thin reserve is a tower that will levy a special assessment, and the assessment lands on whoever owns the fraction when the vote passes, not on whoever owned it when the wear occurred.

## Does Sete Rios work as a rental address?

Sete Rios is a transport interchange rather than a destination, and that is precisely its rental logic. The location joins metro, mainline rail and the long-distance bus terminal, which suits a tenant who commutes rather than a tenant who wants a neighbourhood. Demand is steady and unromantic: professionals who value the connection and price sensitivity over an address with a name.

That profile argues for twelve-month letting rather than short stays. Alojamento Local economics depend on tourist proximity, and Sete Rios is not where visitors choose to stay, so underwrite this asset on long-term rent at 25% autonomous tax and treat any short-let upside as speculative. The mechanics are in [long-term versus holiday rental](/guides/long-term-vs-holiday-rental-portugal/) and the yield arithmetic in [how to calculate rental yield](/guides/how-to-calculate-rental-yield-portugal/).

## What does a non-resident pay to complete?

On a €650,000 apartment a non-resident completing after 1 September 2026 pays flat 7.5% IMT under DL 97/2026, which is €48,750, plus €5,200 stamp duty at 0.8% and roughly €13,000 to €19,500 in legal, notary and registry costs. Cash to close therefore sits near €67,000 to €73,500 above the price.

The IMT is recoverable. DL 97/2026 refunds it where the buyer becomes Portuguese tax resident within 24 months of the purchase, which turns a €48,750 cost into a timing question for anyone already contemplating a move. The refund mechanics and the evidence required are set out in the [IMT refund guide](/guides/imt-refund-tax-resident-portugal/), and the full cost stack in [cost of buying property](/guides/cost-of-buying-property-portugal/).

At this price point AIMI is usually not triggered for a single owner, because the €600,000 allowance applies to taxable value rather than purchase price and the VPT commonly sits below the market figure. Check the caderneta rather than assuming either way.

| Advantages | Disadvantages |

| Condominium accounts and reserve fund readable | Sete Rios is functional rather than characterful |

## How does a 2020s tower compare with older Lisbon stock at the same money?

At €650,000 a buyer in Lisbon chooses between a recent tower apartment and an older, larger flat in a characterful parish, and the choice is mostly about where the money goes after completion. Recent construction carries a high energy class, sound insulation and plant that has not yet aged, which keeps running costs and repair surprises low for the first decade. Older stock at the same price usually offers more floor area and a better address, and asks for a renovation budget.

| Factor | Infinity, recent tower | Older Lisbon flat, same budget |
|---|---|---|
| Energy class | Typically A or B, low bills | Often C to E |
| Floor area for the money | Smaller | Larger |
| Immediate capex | None expected | €600 to €1,200 per m² if renovating |
| Condominium plant | Modern, under warranty | Ageing lift and risers |
| Short-let potential | Weak at Sete Rios | Depends on parish and licence |

The renovation route can pay: works invoiced within 12 years before a sale are deductible against capital gains, and construction contracts on qualifying housing now attract 6% VAT rather than 23% under DL 97/2026. The comparison is worked through in [renovation costs per square metre](/guides/renovation-costs-portugal-per-m2/) and the [6% VAT guide](/guides/portugal-6-percent-vat-housing-works/).

## Who should look at Infinity, and who should not?

Infinity suits a buyer who wants recent construction, an occupancy licence already in hand and a tenant profile that does not depend on tourism. It suits poorly a buyer whose model needs short-let income, or one unwilling to read condominium accounts closely, because in a tower those accounts are the investment case rather than a formality.

Buyers comparing this against the same developer's unfinished schemes should read the completed [Castilho 203](/projects/castilho-203/) as the specification benchmark and the [Vanguard Properties review](/developers/vanguard-properties-portugal/) for pipeline context. For the general framework on new construction versus older stock, see [new-build versus resale](/compare/new-build-vs-resale-property-portugal/).

## What to verify before you offer

1. Two years of condominium accounts, with the reserve fund balance stated
2. Minutes of the last two general meetings, for approved and pending works
3. Lift and pressurisation maintenance contracts and latest inspection certificates
4. Certidão permanente confirming clean title on the fraction
5. Caderneta predial with current VPT, for IMI and any AIMI exposure
6. Parking and storage deeded to the fraction rather than allocated informally
7. Three escritura comparables in the parish through your own lawyer
